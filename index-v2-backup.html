What your site is costing you — Bumble Breeze

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone with a broken AC or a leak would. Your phone is a call link on every page, there is a real online booking pop-up, your license numbers are in the footer, and all 46 pages I opened load. Checked October 15, 2026

What I found

01

Your phone number shows up differently depending on where you look.

The page code lists (702) 674-9775 on every call link. The homepage description Google shows says “Call (702) 703-1346”, and your business markup lists (702) 703-6855. After the page loads in a browser, the number at the top changed between my visits: (702) 703-6855, then (702) 703-6887, then (702) 703-6807. On 15 of the 34 inner pages I saved, links inside the paragraphs dial (702) 703-1346. On your AC repair page, six of those links change their on-screen number but still dial 703-1346. This may be call tracking set up on purpose. I can't tell which numbers are intentional, and I didn't dial any.

02

The homepage is very heavy in the load test.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 11.3 seconds and 12.2 seconds on two runs, and its largest part at 38.2 and 38.9 seconds. The AC repair page gave 11.4 and 38.6 seconds. The home page sends about 7 MB in 204 requests. About 5.5 MB of it comes from 25 outside sources, and the booking and chat service alone sends about 4 MB, including one 2.6 MB file. Your own files are about 1.6 MB. On a fast connection in the same run, the page drew in about 1.3 seconds, so how it feels depends on the phone and the signal.

03

The “Schedule Now” buttons only work if an outside script loads.

The buttons on the homepage have no link address; they open the booking pop-up through a script from another company. My check flagged 5 of them as not crawlable. When I tried one, the pop-up opened and walked through four steps: Issue, Customer, Schedule, Confirm. I didn't fill it out or send anything. If that script is slow or blocked, the buttons do nothing.

04

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your homepage 89 and your AC repair page 84. It found white text on the orange and blue buttons that is hard to read (contrast 2.96 to 3.37, where 4.5 is the minimum), “CALL NOW” links at 3.12, and dark grey text on purple in the membership block at 1.26. It also found two header links and the pop-up's close button with no readable name, and 5 links that only say “Learn More”.

05

Your emergency page has no main heading, and you have two accessibility pages.

The 24/7 emergency page has no H1 heading; the other 45 pages I opened each have one. /accessibility-statement/ is titled “Accessibility Statement for CampDigital”, while /about-us/accessibility-statement/ is titled with your name.

06

Behind the scenes, a few details are off.

On the 14 service pages and one blog post I saved, the author in the page markup is an email address instead of a person or your business. The service pages have a “Frequently Asked Questions” section but no FAQ markup; only the FAQ page has it. 40 of the 46 titles are longer than 60 characters (the longest is 92), and 12 of the 46 pages, including the homepage, have no preview image set. Your footer and your markup also point to different Facebook, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What's working

Your phone is a tap-to-call link on every page I opened, and there is a real online booking pop-up that opens and walks through four steps. Your three contractor license numbers are in the footer. All 46 pages I opened load, and each has a title, description and canonical address. No image on those pages is missing its description. Your sitemap works and lists 198 blog posts, there are service-area pages for 20 communities plus six city pages, and there are pages for financing, offers, reviews, FAQ and staff. Your office hours of 6:45 am to 10:00 pm daily match between the contact page and your markup. Layout shift is low (0.043 on the homepage). In my searches, your own name brought up four of your own pages.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, real-visitor speed, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), what each phone number does when called, whether the forms and booking complete (I didn't submit any), your Google listing, and speed on pages other than the homepage and AC repair page. I opened 46 pages. I didn't open 102 of the 147 pages in your sitemap or 197 of your 198 blog posts. I didn't look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks /wp-admin/ and a form-upload folder, asks crawlers to wait 10 seconds and names your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    A working sitemap

    /sitemap.xml redirects to /sitemap_index.xml, which lists four sitemaps: 147 pages, 198 posts, 6 services and a locations file. /wp-sitemap.xml returns “not found”.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

  • Gap

    Detailed business markup

    The street address (4755 W. University Ave.) is in the markup but not in the visible text of the pages I read. The footer and the markup link to different Facebook, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

  • Gap
    Author in the markup is an email address

  • Gap
